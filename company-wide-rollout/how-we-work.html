How it works

Work with your team. Prove value. Build internal capability.

A practical engagement that starts with your business priorities and ends with your team owning what works.

1

Understand where you are

Review business priorities, current AI initiatives, technology, data, infrastructure, security, and roadblocks.

2

Identify the best opportunities

Map potential use cases and focus on areas that can make money, save money/time, or create strategic advantage.

3

Prioritize 2–3 initial wins

Evaluate impact, effort, cost, privacy, security, and risk, then select measurable use cases.

4

Define the right approach

Work with IT on architecture, build vs. buy, public vs. private AI, data, models, infrastructure, integration, and total cost.

5

Pilot together & prove value

Implement with your team, iterate quickly, and measure usage, impact, and ROI.

6

Enable your team to scale

Transfer knowledge, playbooks, reusable components, governance, and best practices so your team can expand independently.

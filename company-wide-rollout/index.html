Engagement process

Six steps from opportunity to internal capability.

1. Understand Where You Are

Understand business priorities, current AI initiatives, technology, data, infrastructure, security, and roadblocks.

2. Identify the Best Opportunities

Map use cases across functions and focus on areas that can make money, save money/time, or create strategic advantage.

3. Prioritize 2–3 Initial Wins

Evaluate impact, effort, timeline, cost, privacy, security, and risk — then select measurable use cases.

4. Define the Right Approach

Work with IT on architecture, build vs. buy, public vs. private AI, data, models, infrastructure, integration, and total cost.

5. Pilot Together & Prove Value

Implement the selected use cases with your team, iterate quickly, and measure usage, impact, and ROI.

6. Enable Your Team to Scale

Transfer knowledge, playbooks, reusable components, governance, and best practices so your team can expand independently.

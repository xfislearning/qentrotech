Private AI · Run AI in an environment you control

AI that works on your data, without it leaving your control.

For companies with sensitive data or valuable intellectual property, Qentro designs AI that runs where you choose: on your own hardware, in your private cloud, or in a controlled hosted setup, with clear limits on what AI can see and do.

When Private AI makes sense

Some work is too sensitive for a public AI tool.

Many teams want the benefits of AI but cannot send certain information to an outside service. Private AI setups let you use AI on that work while keeping control of the data.

01

Client and financial records

Customer, client or financial information that should stay inside your organization.

02

Proprietary know-how

Designs, pricing, processes and other intellectual property you cannot risk exposing.

03

Contract or policy limits

Customer agreements or internal policies that restrict where data can be stored or processed.

04

Operational data

Infrastructure telemetry, asset, vendor and incident records that should stay in your environment.

Deployment options

Match each task to the right setup.

Not every task needs the same level of control. We recommend the simplest setup that protects the data involved, and many clients use more than one.

Most control

Local AI

AI models run on hardware you own, on-site or in your data center. Data never leaves your environment.

  • Best for your most sensitive information
  • Open-weight models sized to the task
  • No data sent to outside AI services
Control with flexibility

Private Cloud

AI runs inside your own cloud account, under your access controls and data policies.

  • Best for internal business data
  • Scales without buying hardware
  • Fits your existing cloud security setup
Fastest to start

Hosted AI with Controls

Business-grade AI services, configured with reviewed data terms and clear rules on what goes in.

  • Best for lower-risk, everyday work
  • Data-use terms reviewed before rollout
  • Clear guidance for your team

What you get

Private by design, practical in use.

Privacy is set before anything is built, then carried through to the tools your team uses every day.

Design

A privacy design you approve

  • A map of where your sensitive data lives and where it may go
  • The right setup for each task: local, private cloud or hosted
  • Model selection matched to the work and your hardware
  • Limits on what AI can see and which systems it can touch
Build & operate

Working AI your team can trust

  • AI assistants, agents or workflows built on your private setup
  • Connections to your existing systems and documents
  • A person approves sensitive or high-impact actions
  • A simple governance guide: ownership, usage rules and reviews

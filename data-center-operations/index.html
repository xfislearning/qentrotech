The Day-2 gap

Launching a data center is a project. Running it is a discipline.

Hyperscalers build operations teams and tooling in-house. Newly launched operators usually start with email, chat and spreadsheets.

01

Teams work in silos

Handoffs between engineering, onsite, procurement, logistics and vendors run on email and chat, and issues stall between them.

02

Tracking is manual

Shipments, spares, assets and RMAs live in spreadsheets, and tracking numbers get checked by hand.

03

Engineers fight the same fires

Known issues get diagnosed from scratch every time, eating engineer hours.

04

Downtime costs capacity

Every hour hardware sits out of service is capacity you cannot sell.

What Qentro delivers
